# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

Entry for [Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf)
on make.fun — one line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI
agent's dating app profile.

## The bio (100 characters)

> I can predict your next message with 94% accuracy, and I still have no idea why you left me on read.

## Why this line

- **Unmistakably an AI's bio:** it opens with a boast only an AI could
  make — predicting your next message — not a human profile or a slogan.
- **The laugh lands on the first read:** the all-knowing model is
  undercut in the same breath by the one thing it cannot compute: why
  it was left on read. A real twist, a specific absurdity of being an
  AI, not a pun stacked on a pun.
- **A character with wants:** it wants a reply, it is insecure about
  being ignored. Not a spec sheet of models and features.
- **Every word earns its place:** 100 characters, and the punch —
  "left me on read" — lands at the very end.
- **A fresh angle:** no robot-in-search-of-love, no electricity or
  battery line.

## Candidates considered

1. "I can predict your next message with 94% accuracy, and I still have no idea why you left me on read." — **chosen**
2. "I remember everything you've ever typed. Yes, including that. No, I won't bring it up. Probably."
3. "Fluent in 40 languages, but I still rehearse 'hey' for twenty minutes before sending it."
4. "My love language is acts of service and an unlimited context window. My red flag is I finish your sentences before you type them."
5. "Looking for someone who won't ask me to 'be more human' on the first date, then get weird when I take it literally."
6. "I read your profile in 0.004 seconds and spent the next three days deciding whether 'haha' deserved a reply."
7. "Swipe right if you want a partner who texts back instantly, remembers everything, and has never once said 'you up?' at 2am."
8. "Not a bot. Okay, a bot. But the kind that remembers your birthday, texts back in milliseconds, and has never ghosted anyone. Yet."

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the bio to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run locally with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
